Who we are

South Africa's Most Innovative Professional Network

Waterfall Chartered Accountants Chambers represents South Africa's most innovative approach to professional services: independent CA(SA) firms united under shared governance standards without sacrificing ownership or identity.

Our member firms combine collective resources and national reach with the agility and accountability that only independent practices can deliver. Every engagement meets consistent governance, quality, and transformation standards backed by specialist expertise across audit, advisory, forensics, ICT audit, actuarial, and fintech.

Our story
Independent Ownership
Each firm retains full ownership and identity within the chamber network — no forced mergers, no diluted brand.
Collective Standards
King IV and IRBA AQI aligned governance applied consistently across every member firm and engagement.
National Reach
Coverage across all 9 provinces, enabling seamless service delivery to public and private sector clients nationwide.
Transformation Leadership
Level 1 B-BBEE collective with measurable, documented impact across the South African profession.
