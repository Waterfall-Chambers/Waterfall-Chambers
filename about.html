Our Institution

A different approach to professional services.

Waterfall Chartered Accountants Chambers represents a collaborative approach to professional services, bringing together independent CA(SA) practices and specialist professional capabilities under a shared institutional framework.

Rather than operating as a conventional single-firm structure, the Chambers provides a platform through which independent practices can collaborate, access broader capabilities and participate in a collective professional environment.

Each participating practice retains its own ownership, management and professional identity. Through common governance principles, shared standards and collaboration, the Chambers supports a coordinated approach to delivering professional services across South Africa.

Our Evolution

Our Journey

The development of Waterfall Chambers reflects an ongoing commitment to building a collaborative, independent and future-focused professional services institution.

2022

Waterfall Chartered Accountants Chambers was founded by a team of CA(SA) professionals, engineers and strategic leaders with a vision to develop a collaborative professional chambers model. From the outset, the objective was to bring independent practices together through shared institutional principles while preserving their autonomy and ownership.

2023

The Chambers expanded its professional network across multiple provinces, strengthening its institutional governance, quality and compliance frameworks. Transformation initiatives and collaborative professional development became increasingly important elements of the model.

2024

Waterfall Chambers secured its first corporate clients and advanced its institutional capabilities. The organisation continued developing its collaborative model, extending professional reach and supporting specialist capabilities across audit, advisory and forensic services.

2025

The Chambers continued strengthening its transformation positioning and expanding specialist capabilities in areas including fintech, actuarial services, ICT audit and regulatory compliance. Its focus remained on combining independent professional expertise with collective institutional capability.

2026 & Beyond

Waterfall Chambers continues to develop its national professional network, strengthen collaborative relationships and expand future-focused services. The long-term direction is centred on building an institution where independent professional practices can access broader opportunities while maintaining their distinct identities.
